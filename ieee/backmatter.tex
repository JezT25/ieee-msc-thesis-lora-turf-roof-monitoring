\section{Public ThingSpeak Channels}\label{app:thingspeak-channels}
The measurements from the Keldur deployment are published on four public ThingSpeak channels under the author account \texttt{jvt6hiis}, one channel per sensor node. They provide air temperature, relative humidity, soil temperature, raw soil moisture readings, and battery voltage, which can be viewed online or exported. The sensor-node and base-station firmware is available at \url{https://github.com/JezT25/turfroof_lora}.
